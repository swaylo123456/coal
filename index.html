About Ironvale

Built On Depth Of Experience.

For over two decades, Ironvale Coal & Minerals has supplied thermal, industrial and metallurgical coal to power plants, manufacturers and heavy industry across the region. Every seam we mine, every tonne we move, is handled under strict safety and quality protocols.

Our integrated model — mining, processing, transportation and supply — means fewer handoffs, tighter quality control, and delivery timelines our partners can build their own operations around.
